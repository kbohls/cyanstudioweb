  This reproduction print documents a monumental event - the herring spawn. Each
  spring, herring return to nearshore waters to spawn. Female herring lay roe
  (in the millions) while males release milt to fertilize the eggs, turning the
  water a milky blue. This image was created using strands of seagrass covered
  in herring roe, that were collected from wrack. They are double exposed with
  an image of the spawn taken in Ucluelet, BC. 


  Each card comes with an envelope and protective sleeve. Cards are printed on archival quality paper, and could be framed as a 5x7 fine art print, in addition to serving as a gift / greeting card.
